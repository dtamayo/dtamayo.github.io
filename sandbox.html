The Driven Pendulum as a Model for Chaos

A pendulum whose pivot is shaken up and down once per unit time. Each term in $\dot v$ is a wave, and each dial shows that wave's phase as seen by the pendulum. Click the phase plane to launch an orbit.

$$\dot\theta = v, \qquad \dot v = -\alpha\sin\theta - \epsilon\left[\sin(\theta-2\pi t)+\sin(\theta+2\pi t)\right]$$
